Your Learning Journey

Choose any stage to view or update your work. Checkmarks indicate recorded activities, not mastery.

Unstuck guides you from noticing confusion to identifying knowledge gaps, choosing learning strategies, evaluating what worked, and adjusting your next steps. You can move through the stages at your own pace.

STAGE 01

Capture

In class and short on time? Jot down a few keywords now and add details later.

Capturing confusion early preserves information you can return to later, even when you are not ready to explain the problem.

A keyword, slide number, or short note can help you recall this moment later.

STAGE 02

Clarify

Identify what you know and pinpoint where your understanding breaks down.

Metacognitive monitoring helps you recognize what you understand and identify the specific gaps that need attention.

STEP 1

💡 Start with one small thing you know, such as a related concept, an opposite idea, or an example you've seen.

STAGE 04

Reflect

Look back at what you actually tried, evaluate what helped, and decide what to do next.

Evaluation and adaptation help you recognize which strategies were useful and adjust your learning decisions based on the results.

STEP 1

Which strategies did you actually use, and which helped?

Select what you really tried, even if it wasn't part of your original plan. Then identify which approaches helped you most.

A. Actually Used B. Helped Me
STEP 2

How much time did you spend?

Estimate the time you actively spent working on this confusion, not the time since you first recorded it.

STEP 3

What would you do differently next?

Use what you've learned to decide whether to change your approach, revisit the knowledge gap, or continue practicing.
